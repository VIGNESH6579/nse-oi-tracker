# Fixes applied 2026-10-06 (free-tier)

## 1. Stuck `startup_state: BLOCKED` while `readiness: READY`

**Root cause**  
Render Free cold-starts often happen outside market hours. The background
startup path ran a `pre_open` self-test. A transient critical failure
(universe not yet loaded, bars still downloading, brief Angel race) set
`_startup_state = "BLOCKED"` permanently. Later a successful `post_open`
self-test updated only `self_test.readiness()` (so `/api/health` showed
`readiness: READY`) but never cleared `_startup_state`.

**Code changes (`app/main.py`)**

- `_startup_backfill_then_retest`: off-hours pre_open BLOCKED is recorded as
  `DEGRADED` instead of permanent `BLOCKED`. Backfill timeout/failure is also
  DEGRADED when the market is closed.
- Lifespan final readiness assignment: same off-hours BLOCKED → DEGRADED rule.
- `scheduled_readiness_recheck`: now recovers a stale BLOCKED/DEGRADED
  `startup_state` to READY whenever any self-test stage has produced READY
  (runs recovery even when the market is closed). Only demotes READY →
  BLOCKED/DEGRADED during market hours.
